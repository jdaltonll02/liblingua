# LibLingua Pilot Benefit-Sharing Policy

Version: 1.0  
Status: pilot framework; compensation amounts require a separate recorded decision

Language communities retain a decision-making role through community approval of metadata, guidelines, gold references, and releases. The project will report dataset use, corrections, and access restrictions back to the relevant community representatives.

Before a public or commercial release, the project must document:

- contributor compensation or reimbursement arrangements;
- community capacity-building or infrastructure commitments;
- attribution preferences and community naming conventions;
- how revenue or commercial benefit, if any, will be shared;
- who can request correction, restriction, or retraction on the community's behalf.

No public or commercial release is allowed while these items remain undecided.
