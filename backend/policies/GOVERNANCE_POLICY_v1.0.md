# LibLingua Research Governance Policy

Version: 1.0  
Status: Approved project direction; activation requires recorded community and research approvals  
Approved direction date: 2026-11-09

## Scope

LibLingua will begin as a closed, supervised research pilot involving adults aged 18 or older. No public dataset release or commercial redistribution is permitted during the pilot.

Each language variety must have a recorded community authority and research authority. Both authorities must approve the variety definition, orthography, annotation guideline, gold references, and each release candidate. A rejection or withdrawal by either authority prevents activation or release.

## Release rule

A release must be immutable, versioned, reproducible, and auditable. It must contain only records with valid source rights, active participant consent, complete linguistic metadata, independent review, and a leakage-safe split assignment. Audio is controlled-access only.

## Corrections and withdrawal

Participant withdrawals affect all future exports and releases. Published versions are not silently rewritten: affected versions are restricted or retracted and receive a public correction record without exposing participant identity.

## Oversight

The project must retain the names, account identifiers, authority roles, decisions, and dates of release approvers. Ethics or institutional approval must not be claimed unless separately documented.
