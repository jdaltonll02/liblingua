# LibLingua Acceptable and Prohibited Use Policy

Version: 1.0  
Applies to: all dataset access tiers

Permitted pilot use is limited to approved language documentation, language-technology research, quality evaluation, and community-approved educational work.

The following are prohibited:

- identifying, re-identifying, contacting, profiling, or locating contributors;
- voice cloning, speaker impersonation, biometric identification, or surveillance;
- discriminatory classification, policing, immigration enforcement, military targeting, or political persuasion;
- generating deceptive content presented as a contributor or community member;
- inferring sensitive traits not explicitly supplied for an approved research purpose;
- redistributing controlled audio or access credentials;
- removing provenance, consent, licence, correction, or retraction notices;
- using pilot data commercially or releasing it publicly before a later approved licence explicitly allows that use.

Access may be suspended for suspected violations. Restrictions continue to apply to copies and derived artifacts.
