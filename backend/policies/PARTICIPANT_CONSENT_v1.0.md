# LibLingua Adult Participant Information and Consent

Version: 1.0  
Pilot: closed research pilot  
Eligibility: adults aged 18 or older

## What participation involves

Participants may translate English prompts, optionally record the target-language translation, optionally read the English prompt aloud, and provide language-background information needed to interpret the annotations.

Participation is voluntary. A participant may skip a prompt or stop contributing. Each use is chosen separately: written contribution, target-language audio, English audio, model training, public release, commercial use, and future research.

## Current pilot limits

The pilot does not authorize public or commercial redistribution. Audio is treated as potentially identifying and is available only through controlled access when the participant has granted the relevant audio scope.

## Review and privacy

Contributions may be reviewed by qualified reviewers and community/research leads. Public research data must use pseudonymous identifiers and must not expose account details or direct demographic identifiers.

## Withdrawal

Participants may withdraw individual scopes from the Consent & Data page. Withdrawal immediately excludes the affected material from new exports. If material is already present in a frozen release, the project records a correction, restriction, or retraction rather than silently changing the cited version.

This document is project consent information, not a claim of institutional ethics approval.
